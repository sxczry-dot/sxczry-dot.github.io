JC120GE 双开门嵌入式酒柜Dual-Door Embedded Wine Cabinet

美的集团股份有限公司Midea Group · 2025.07 - 2025.09 · 参与人Participant

本项目基于 60L 酒柜进行双箱胆扩容，构建物理隔离式双腔体（总容 120L），通过分路冷量供给及背部双风门隔离风道，同时搭配差异化门体（双层/三层玻璃），以实现两温区精准独立控温，适配不同酒类需求，并结合交错层架提升空间利用率，达成零串温、高能效的模块化储酒系统。

This project expanded the dual-cavity capacity from 60L to 120L with a physically isolated dual-chamber system. Through split-path cooling supply and rear dual-damper isolated air ducts, combined with differentiated door designs (double/triple-layer glass), it achieves precise independent temperature control for two zones, catering to different wine storage needs. Interleaved shelving improves space utilization, resulting in a zero-cross-contamination, high-efficiency modular wine storage system.

The project focused on the following core topics:

  • 交错层架设计提升空间利用率
  • 零串温、高能效模块化储酒系统集成
  • Dual-cavity expansion design and structural optimization
  • Physically isolated dual-chamber construction and split-path cooling supply design
  • Rear dual-damper isolated air duct validation
  • Differentiated door design (double/triple-layer glass) comparison and selection
  • Interleaved shelving design for improved space utilization
  • Zero-cross-contamination, high-efficiency modular wine storage system integration
