About

I currently work at the Technical Management Office of the 7th Management District, Linpan Oil Production Plant, Sinopec Shengli Oilfield Company.

I earned my M.Eng. in Mechanical Engineering from Anhui University, where my research focused on machine vision-based precision position measurement for linear motors.

I received my B.Eng. in Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation from Shandong University of Technology.

My research focuses on machine vision measurement techniques, image processing algorithm optimization, and structural design and simulation. I have published two SCI papers and have one invention patent under substantive examination.

In my spare time, I enjoy table tennis (six years of training experience) and long-distance running.
